Week-wise: how the results moved

Cuts use the audit canvas date (IST), because case-created dates are not recorded in a consistent field across canvases. Only two audit weeks exist so far, so treat this as a baseline, not a trend line. The jump in week 2 is mostly selection: the 16 to 18 Nov audits are single cases picked on request and use the newer Opportunity schema. It does not show that quality got worse.
